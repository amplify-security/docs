## The Organization page

Click **Organization** in the sidebar. The page has five tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | The organization's name and settings |
| **Members** | Who belongs to the organization, and invitations |
| **API keys** | API keys owned by the organization rather than by a person |
| **Billing and Credits** | Credit balance, payment method, and automatic credit purchasing — see [Billing](/console/billing) |
| **Usage & Invoices** | The usage, invoice, and credit dashboards — see [Billing](/console/billing#usage-and-invoices) |

## Members

**Members** lists everyone in the organization with their role.

- **Invite a teammate.** Open **Organization**, choose **Members**, and invite them by email address,
  choosing the role they'll have. The invitation is pending until they accept it from the email they
  receive; once they sign in, they're a member and can use everything the role allows.
- **Change a role or remove a member.** Each member row has a menu to change their role or remove them from
  the organization.

<Note>
  A teammate who signs in before being invited sees *This account is not a member of any organization*.
  Invite them from **Members** and have them sign in again.
</Note>

## What needs an organization admin

Most of Console is open to every member: chat, projects, agents, detections, workflows, runs, findings,
source-control connections, and vendor connectors. A few actions require the organization **Admin** role:

| Action | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Connecting an app under **Agent Tools** — Slack, Atlassian, Linear, Notion, Asana, Google — and disconnecting one | **Connections** → **Add connection**; non-admins see the apps marked *Admin only* |
| Choosing which files an app may read, for apps that grant access per file | The app's card on **Connections** |
| Adding credit, changing the payment method, and turning automatic credit purchasing on or off | **Organization** → **Billing and Credits** |
| Reading the credit balance, payment method, and the usage and invoice dashboards | **Organization** → **Billing and Credits** and **Usage & Invoices** — non-admins see *Unavailable* |

See [connecting an app](/integrations/connecting) and [Billing](/console/billing).

## Organization API keys

The **API keys** tab holds keys that belong to the organization rather than to one person. The keys the CLI
signs in with are personal — create those under [Profile → API keys](/console/profile#api-keys).

## Working in more than one organization

The web console works in one organization at a time — every page shows that organization's projects,
findings, and settings. There is no organization switcher in the web console today.

The CLI can switch: `/context <org>` changes the organization a session works against. See
[the CLI](/interactive/cli#slash-commands).
